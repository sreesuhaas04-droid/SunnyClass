Everything a live class needs

Built on a FastAPI backend with PostgreSQL, WebRTC mesh video, and WebSocket signaling.

👤

Auto attendance by face

face-api.js builds a 128-d descriptor in the browser; the server matches it against the roster and marks you present. Video never leaves your device.

🎫

Roll-number gated rooms

Four checks run before the meeting opens: valid session, roll number on file, enrolled in this class, and a face that matches that roll number.

👁️

Attention tracking

68-point landmarks give eye-aspect-ratio for drowsiness and gaze direction for looking away. Scores are smoothed, so a blink costs nothing.

🔒

Focus lock

Enforced fullscreen, tab-switch detection, and a wake lock. Every infraction is logged to the session and shown to the teacher live.

📶

Low-bandwidth by design

Peer-to-peer mesh means no media server hop. The quality ladder steps from 720p down to audio-only + captions as the network or room size demands.

☀️

SUNNY AI

Grounded in your real attendance rows and the live transcript. Connect any LLM key and it answers study questions too — with a free offline fallback.

📝

Captions & recordings

Speech-to-text broadcast over the class socket, stored as a searchable transcript, and attached to the uploaded recording.

📊

Visual attendance

Donut charts, weekly bars, a 90-day heatmap and a per-student grid — all computed server-side from real records.

📱

Every device

Responsive from a 360px phone to a desktop, with landscape handling, safe-area insets, and a theme that follows your account.
